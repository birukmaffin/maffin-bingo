# MAFFIN BINGO — User manual

## 0. Activation
The first time, the app shows a **Device ID**. Send it to Maffin Technologies (Telegram @Birukmaffin / 0944686822 / 0941681598) and paste the activation code you receive. Each device needs its own code.

## 1. First start
Create a 4–8 digit **operator PIN**. You are shown an 8-digit **recovery code once** — write it down; it is the only way to reset a forgotten PIN. The PIN protects: Settings, editing price/commission, voiding a sale, restarting a draw, cancelling a game, restoring a backup. After you enter it, it stays unlocked for 5 minutes.

## 2. Language
Tap **አማርኛ / English** on the home screen or in Settings. All menus, buttons, messages and instructions switch; called numbers are also shown in Amharic words.

## 3. Running a game
1. **Generate Cards** → *Generate* (e.g. 100). Each card gets a permanent number and unique random numbers. *Print cards* gives A4 pages (2, 4, 6 or 8 per page, black-and-white friendly, with card number).
2. **New Game** → check price (min 20 ETB) and commission (default 20 %). To change them tap *Edit (PIN)*. Tap *Create game*.
3. **Sell cards** → player name, then either a quantity (next unsold cards are used; new ones are generated if needed) or specific IDs like `5, 8-12`. A card cannot be sold twice in one game. Wrong sale? *Void sale* (PIN) — only before the game starts.
4. **Start game** (sales lock). On the game screen:
   * **Draw next number** — secure random, never repeats. *Auto-call* draws every N seconds (Settings).
   * **Call number** — type the number from your physical ball (1–75). Invalid or already-called numbers are refused.
   * Every call is announced by voice, highlighted on the board and added to the history. *Repeat* says the last number again; *Announce only* says any number without recording it.
5. **Verify Bingo** → enter the card ID → *Check*. The app shows the card, the winning line(s) and the player. If the card completed its line on an *earlier* call than the latest, it warns you that this is a late claim. *Confirm winner* ends the game and records the prize.
6. Winning patterns: any horizontal, vertical or the two diagonals; centre is FREE.

## 4. Money
Revenue = cards sold × price. Organizer commission (default 20 %) and winner prize (80 %) are calculated automatically; 100 cards × 20 = 2,000 → 400 commission, 1,600 prize. Prices are frozen per game, so later price changes never alter old records.

## 5. History, players, reports
* **Game History** — search by game ID, player or winner; filter by status and dates; tap a game to see sales, called numbers and the winner.
* **Manage Players** — automatic list of buyers with totals.
* **Financial Reports** — today / 7 / 30 days / month / all / custom; daily table, per-game table, historical revenue; **Export CSV** (opens in Excel) and **Print report**. Cancelled games are excluded from revenue. A game ended without a winner shows its pool under *Unclaimed pools*.

## 6. Voice
Settings → Voice: on/off, English or Amharic, volume, speed, *Test*. Amharic speech needs an Amharic text-to-speech voice on the device; if there is none, the app tells you, speaks English instead and still shows Amharic text.

## 7. Backup & restore — please do this daily
Settings → **Back up now** saves a `.json` file; copy it to a USB stick or cloud. **Restore backup** replaces all data on the device (PIN required, with a confirmation). The home screen reminds you if the last backup is older than a day.

## 7b. Winner alert, TV and contact
* **Winner alert:** when a card completes a line, a green panel appears (sound + vibration) with the card, player and line. **The game keeps running** — when the player calls "Bingo" tap **Verify** and **Confirm winner**. (Optional: Settings → pause auto-call when a card has a line.)
* **TV mode:** big ball, board and prize for the customers. Android: mirror the phone screen to the TV (Smart View/Cast) or use an HDMI adapter. Windows: *Second screen* opens a full-screen display on the TV. In TV mode you can draw with the small controls (or press Space).
